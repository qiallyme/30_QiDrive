# CHAPTER 3 — When Silence Gets Misinterpreted

# There are moments when silence becomes the villain before I even get a chance to speak.

# I go quiet for a second — because I’m thinking, because I’m overwhelmed, because I need breath — and the energy around me shifts instantly.

# Someone assumes I’m mad.

# Someone else assumes I’m judging them.

# Another person assumes I’m ignoring them.

# My silence becomes a blank screen they project their deepest insecurities onto.

# I pause to process — they feel abandoned.

# I regulate my breathing — they feel rejected.

# I withdraw for a moment — they feel dismissed.

# Suddenly the story being told about me becomes louder than anything I actually feel.

# Quietness, for me, is not rejection.

# It is recalibration.

# But the world treats silence like betrayal.

# People demand instant responses, complete transparency, emotional availability on command.

# And when I cannot provide it — not because I don’t care, but because my nervous system is working overtime — they fill the gaps with their own fears.

# You’re distant.

# You’re upset.

# You’re pulling away.

# You’re not communicating.

# They don’t see the storm in my mind, the pressure in my chest, the noise in my ribs. They only see the stillness.

# Silence becomes suspicion.

# And I become misunderstood all over again.

# Not because I am unclear, but because the world is louder than my ability to breathe.
