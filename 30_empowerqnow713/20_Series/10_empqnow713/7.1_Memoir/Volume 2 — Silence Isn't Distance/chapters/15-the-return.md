# **CHAPTER 15 — The Return**

Rebuilding After Collapse

Coming back after collapse is strange.

You’re the same person, but not the same.

Softened.

Clearer.

More cautious with your energy.

The world looks different when you return.

Less urgent.

Less demanding.

Less entitled to your constant availability.

You start noticing things:

You don’t rush to answer messages.

You don’t absorb every emotion in the room.

You don’t overfunction by default.

You don’t apologize for resting.

You don’t explain your quiet.

You begin choosing instead of reacting.

Protecting instead of performing.

Feeling instead of fawning.

Healing doesn’t mean you never fall again.

It means you fall softer.

You fall slower.

You fall with more awareness of the ground beneath you.

You rebuild gently:

One boundary at a time.

One moment of honesty at a time.

One act of self-protection at a time.

And eventually, the return stops feeling like a comeback

and starts feeling like the life you should have always been allowed to live.

You learn the truth:

Silence isn’t distance.

Stillness isn’t disinterest.

Withdrawal isn’t rejection.

They are the ways your nervous system holds you

until you can hold yourself again.
