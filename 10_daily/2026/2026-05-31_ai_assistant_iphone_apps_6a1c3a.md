## 🧠 The *actual* best setup (today)

Don’t look for one app. That’s the trap.

Use a **2-layer system**:

### Layer 1 — Brain (capture + thinking)
→ ChatGPT (voice)

### Layer 2 — Execution
→ Siri + TickTick (or Apple Reminders)

---

### Example flow (this is what you want):

You say:
> “Hey, remind me next week to follow up with Zai and also note that I already talked to him about the documents”

**Realistic best flow right now:**
1. Say it to ChatGPT → it structures it
2. Tap → send to reminders / copy
3. Siri executes reminder
