Ah, the infamous green screen of digital drama! Basically, what that "Memory_Management" stop code is telling you is that your device ran into a memory-related hiccup that forced it to shut down and reboot. 

In other words, something about how your system was handling memory got a bit out of whack—like a little gremlin in the RAM or a driver going sideways—and your computer decided to throw up that green screen as a kind of “whoops, let’s restart and fix this” signal. 

It’s not usually a huge disaster—just your device’s way of saying, “Hang on, let me sort myself out.”
