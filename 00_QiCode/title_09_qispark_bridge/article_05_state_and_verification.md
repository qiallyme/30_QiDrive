## Article 5. State and Verification

### Sec. 9.05.010. Meaningful Movement

Stable ID: `QIC-09-05-001` (`T09.A05.S010`)

Qi systems should help the person distinguish current state, desired state,
the gap between them, the next meaningful move, the verified result, and the
learning that follows. Collection without orientation or useful movement is
incomplete system behavior.

### Sec. 9.05.020. Visibility

Stable ID: `QIC-09-05-002` (`T09.A05.S020`)

The person must be able to determine what the system did, why it did it, which
source supported it, whether it succeeded, and what remains incomplete.
