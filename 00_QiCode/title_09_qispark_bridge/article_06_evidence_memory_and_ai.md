## Article 6. Evidence, Memory, and AI

### Sec. 9.06.010. Evidence and Provenance

Stable ID: `QIC-09-06-001` (`T09.A06.S010`)

- Preserve original captures and evidence.
- Store AI interpretation separately from its source.
- Every consequential assertion must be traceable to a source.
- Corrections supersede interpretations without destroying source history.

### Sec. 9.06.020. Determinism Before AI

Stable ID: `QIC-09-06-002` (`T09.A06.S020`)

Deterministic services govern persistence, calculations, permissions,
relationships, recurrence, export, restore, and external mutations. AI may
assist extraction, recommendation, explanation, drafting, and proposed
classification, but it does not replace deterministic domain rules.

### Sec. 9.06.030. Memory Governance

Stable ID: `QIC-09-06-003` (`T09.A06.S030`)

Qi systems distinguish working, episodic, semantic, and procedural memory.
Semantic claims carry source, confidence, status, and validity. Sensitive or
consequential facts require approval. Contradictions are superseded explicitly,
not overwritten invisibly.
