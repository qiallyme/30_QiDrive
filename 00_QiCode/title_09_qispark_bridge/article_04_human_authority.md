## Article 4. Human Authority

### Sec. 9.04.010. Final Human Authority

Stable ID: `QIC-09-04-001` (`T09.A04.S010`)

- The person remains the final authority over consequential personal truth,
  priorities, permissions, and action.
- AI may propose, summarize, relate, explain, and draft; it may not silently
  establish consequential personal truth.
- Destructive or externally consequential action requires explicit authority
  scoped to the action being taken.

### Sec. 9.04.020. Proposals Are Not Decisions

Stable ID: `QIC-09-04-002` (`T09.A04.S020`)

- AI-generated classifications, memories, relationships, and plans remain
  proposals until the applicable deterministic rule or person approves them.
- Absence of objection is not authority for a consequential mutation.
